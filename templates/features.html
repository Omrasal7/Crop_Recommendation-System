Features

A quick overview of what Krushi includes and how each part works.

🌱
Crop Prediction

Input 7 soil and climate values to get a crop recommendation from a trained machine learning model. Results show the crop name and why it was suggested.

📋
Crop Details

After prediction, the result page shows growing conditions, useful tips, and a plain-language explanation of why the crop fits the input values.

💬
KrushiBot Chat

A simple rule-based farming assistant. Ask about soil pH, NPK, irrigation, crop seasons, organic farming, pests, and more.

📱
Responsive Design

Works on desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile. No account or registration needed to use the prediction or chat features.

How to Use the Crop Recommendation
  1. 1
    Go to the Predict Crop page from the navigation bar.
  2. 2
    Enter your Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium values from a soil test report or field measurement.
  3. 3
    Enter your local Temperature, Humidity, pH, and Rainfall values.
  4. 4
    Click Recommend Crop to get the prediction result.
  5. 5
    Read the result page for the crop name, reason, growing conditions, and useful tips.
