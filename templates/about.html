Why KRUSHI exists

Built to make agricultural decisions feel less overwhelming.

KRUSHI is a crop recommendation and farming guidance project designed to make useful agricultural intelligence easier to access. Instead of giving farmers and learners a wall of technical data, it turns key field inputs into a focused crop suggestion and pairs that with practical advice.

The goal is simple: help people choose better crops, understand their soil conditions more clearly, and get quick support on everyday farming topics such as irrigation, nutrient balance, season planning, and pest care.

Prediction-first workflow Convert raw field values into a recommendation with a cleaner decision path.
Accessible guidance Keep advice readable for students, growers, and first-time users.
Local mindset Focus on practical agricultural concepts that fit Indian farming contexts.
Better user experience Bring prediction and conversational help into one coherent workspace.
